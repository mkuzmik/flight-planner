✈ Flight Planner

Legend

Navigation

TC
True Course — bearing from A to B relative to true north
DECL
Magnetic declination — difference between true and magnetic north at route centroid
MC
Magnetic Course — TC adjusted for declination (TC − decl)
WCA
Wind Correction Angle — how much to crab into the wind to track the desired course
MH
Magnetic Heading — the heading to fly (MC + WCA)
GS
Ground Speed — actual speed over ground, accounting for wind component
ETE
Estimated Time En-route — leg distance ÷ ground speed
TAS
True Air Speed — airspeed corrected for altitude and temperature

Fuel

Trip
Fuel burned flying all legs at cruise consumption (GPH × ETE)
Taxi
Fixed allowance for taxi and engine run-up before departure
Climb
Extra fuel for one full-power climb; counted twice in total (departure + go-around)
T&G
Touch-and-go: count × (circuit time × GPH + one climb fuel)
Expected burn
Taxi + climb + T&G fuel + trip — the planned consumption with no contingencies
Go-around
Second climb allowance, budgeted for a missed approach at destination
Alternate
Fuel to fly from destination to alternate airport + one go-around
Holding
Fixed 10-minute holding reserve at cruise consumption
Reserve
Regulatory final reserve (45 or 60 min at cruise consumption)
